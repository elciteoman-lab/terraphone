Sound of Change

A visitor can compare before and after, select the listening perspective, apply all or reset. Use real controls, a shared state and immediate synchronized visual, acoustic and index updates. Acoustics and science
Use Web Audio gain controls for traffic (including engines and horns), construction, industry, crowds and nature. Start sound on user interaction. Preserve master volume, mute, smooth transitions and cleanup. Show the source-based 0–100 “Simulated Noise Index”. Distinguish it from measured decibels and reported complaints. Sum acoustic energy logarithmically, avoid counting overlapping interventions twice, and distinguish indoor insulation from outdoor exposure. Hearing-health education alone does not reduce ambient noise. Sections 6–7 give the full model and audio requirements.
